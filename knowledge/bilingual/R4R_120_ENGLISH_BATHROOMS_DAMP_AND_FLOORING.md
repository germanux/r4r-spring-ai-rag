# English renovation Q&A — bathrooms, damp, flooring and outdoor areas

## Why does bathroom mold return after repainting?

Mold can return when moisture remains. Examine condensation, ventilation, leaks and cold surfaces first; correct the source, clean and dry properly, and only then refinish.

## Can I change a bathtub into a walk-in shower?

Often possible, but check drain location and slope, floor depth, waterproofing continuity and entry height. A flush floor is not guaranteed without measurements.

## Do new shower tiles make the shower waterproof?

Tiles and grout are not a substitute for the correct waterproofing system beneath them. Check seals, drains, corners and substrate conditions.

## Can I relocate a toilet?

Feasibility depends on the soil stack, drainage runs, gradients, floor height and structural restrictions. A site assessment is required before promising a new position.

## What is the difference between a floor base and floor finishing?

A solera is a base layer or slab-like supporting layer; a solado is the final flooring finish installed on a prepared base. The terms describe different parts of a floor assembly.

## Can I install new outdoor tiles over existing ones?

Only if the existing system has adequate bond, levels, drainage and compatible substrate. Raised levels can obstruct doors or worsen ponding.

## Why does rainwater collect on my patio?

Possible causes include inadequate falls, blocked drains and problematic thresholds or details. Diagnose the drainage route before choosing a new surface finish.

## Can paint stop rising damp?

Painting alone does not correct a moisture source. Investigate rising moisture, lateral seepage, hidden leaks and salts before selecting a repair.

## Will replacing windows stop condensation?

Not necessarily. Window performance, ventilation, indoor humidity and thermal bridges all matter; more airtight windows still require adequate ventilation.

## How can I tell a roof leak from condensation?

Track when and where the stain occurs, whether it follows rainfall or indoor humidity, and inspect likely roof junctions and cold surfaces. A photo does not establish the cause.

## What is an appropriate floor for a wet changing room?

Select a flooring system with suitable slip resistance, water and cleaning performance for the intended use, not purely appearance. Verify applicable requirements.

## Does the system answer structural safety questions?

It can explain what must be checked, but cannot certify that a wall is non-load-bearing or that a cracked element is safe without competent structural assessment.
