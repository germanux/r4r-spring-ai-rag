# English renovation Q&A — offices, historic buildings and budgets

## Can an office remain open during renovation?

Possibly, if the work area can be segregated and exits, services, accessibility and safe circulation maintained. Plan dust, noise and service interruptions before deciding.

## Can office washrooms be renovated in stages?

Yes, when alternative facilities, isolation of plumbing and user safety are available. The staging plan must not leave occupants without required amenities.

## What should I check before removing a partition wall?

Structural role, services, fire and escape requirements, approvals and potential hazardous materials. Do not assume a narrow partition is automatically safe to remove.

## Can an old stone wall be covered with modern plaster?

The plaster system must be compatible with the wall, salts, moisture and substrate movement. Do not cover active damp without understanding its source.

## Why is my newly renovated wall still damp?

A finish can conceal but not eliminate a leak, condensation problem or rising moisture. Reassess the moisture source and drying before additional coatings.

## Do all renovation projects need planning permission?

Requirements depend on location, type and scope of work. In Spain, check the relevant municipality and applicable technical codes instead of assuming one national answer.

## What should a renovation quotation include?

Scope, measurements, materials, labor, site access, waste handling, exclusions, payment conditions and assumptions. Compare like-for-like items rather than total price alone.

## What happens if hidden damage is found?

Photograph and describe it, explain repair options and costs, and agree on any additional work except urgent safety measures. Keep a written change record.

## Can a renovation improve energy efficiency?

Yes, measures such as insulation, windows and equipment upgrades may help. Savings depend on the actual building, climate and use; exact percentages require evaluation.

## Can you advise on a swimming-pool leak?

The general approach is to identify whether the loss comes from the pool shell, pipework, fittings or evaporation before selecting a repair. A residential renovation corpus may not cover specialized pool systems.

## Is a recorded demonstration equivalent to a live technical inspection?

No. A recorded example illustrates an earlier output; a live model response still requires relevant evidence, and neither replaces onsite checks or professional certification.

## Where does the technical information come from?

The assistant is expected to cite the retrieved renovation documents. A citation should support the specific claim and must not be treated as proof of unrelated details.
