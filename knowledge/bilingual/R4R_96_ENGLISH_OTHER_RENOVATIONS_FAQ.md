# Riansares 4R — English guide: bathrooms, damp, flooring and estimates

## Q — What is the difference between a concrete slab base and floor tiling?

A **solera** is a base or ground-bearing layer, designed for its intended use and loads; **solado** is the final flooring or paving finish, such as tiles or stone. You may replace the finish without replacing a sound base, or rebuild the base if the existing one is unsuitable. Do not confuse the supporting layer with the finish.

## Q — What is the difference between a screed, a slab and a finished floor?

A structural slab or ground-bearing base supports loads, a screed is generally a leveling or receiving layer in appropriate assemblies, and the finished floor is the wearing surface. Spanish construction terminology varies with context: do not assume every "solera" means an English structural slab. Ask about the actual construction system.

## Q — Can moisture-resistant plasterboard waterproof a shower?

No. Moisture-resistant board is not, by itself, a continuous waterproofing system. A shower requires correctly detailed waterproofing at joints, corners, penetrations and transitions to the tray or floor, using a compatible system.

## Q — Why does bathroom mold keep coming back?

The cause of moisture may remain: inadequate ventilation, condensation, cold surfaces or leaks. Clean-up without stopping the moisture source is unlikely to last. [US EPA guidance](https://www.epa.gov/mold/how-do-i-get-rid-mold).

## Q — Can I move the toilet during a bathroom renovation?

Possibly. Drain location, pipe diameter, slopes, floor depth, inspection access and connection to the soil stack must be checked before confirming feasibility or cost.

## Q — What information is needed for a first renovation assessment?

Location, photographs, approximate measurements, intended use, visible defects, expected outcome, desired timeframe and indicative budget. For kitchens and bathrooms, indicate whether water and waste connections will be moved. A first assessment does not replace a site visit.

## Q — Why are two renovation quotes so different?

They may differ in measured scope, preparation of existing surfaces, material quality, service replacements, access, waste, labor, project risks and exclusions. Compare itemized proposals for the same work instead of comparing total figures alone. [NARI](https://remodelingdoneright.nari.org/plan-your-remodel/faqs-glossary).

## Q — Can you remove a wall in an old house?

Do not assume it is non-load-bearing without assessment. Determine whether it supports floors, roofs or other structure, and whether permissions or professional design are required before removal.

## Q — Does breathable lime mortar stop rising damp?

No. It can be compatible with certain old masonry systems, but cannot stop an active water source by itself. The origin and movement of moisture must be assessed, together with salts and substrate conditions.

## Q — Why are slopes important when paving an outdoor patio?

Surface levels and drainage routes affect whether rainwater drains to an appropriate outlet or collects near doors, walls and foundations. Slope and drainage design are specific to the site and construction system.

## Q — Can an office be renovated while people are working?

Sometimes, with suitable phasing, isolation, access and safety precautions, service continuity and compliance with the rules for the building's use. Scheduling must account for noise, dust and possible utility interruptions.

## Q — Can broken pipes cause damp or mold behind kitchen cabinets?

Yes, persistent leaks can wet concealed materials and create conditions for mold. Fix the water source, inspect affected materials, dry thoroughly and replace damaged components when necessary. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home).

## Q — Do all renovation works need a permit?

Permit requirements depend on the country, municipality, property and work involved. For a project in Spain, confirm requirements with the relevant town hall and competent professional. English or US regulations should not be used to declare Spanish permits unnecessary.

## Q — How much does a kitchen renovation cost?

It cannot be priced reliably without the kitchen size, location, finishes, condition of services, scope and access. Historic examples in R4R knowledge are illustrative, not automatically current market quotes or offers. Do not introduce an example 7 m² kitchen price unless the user asks about that scenario.
