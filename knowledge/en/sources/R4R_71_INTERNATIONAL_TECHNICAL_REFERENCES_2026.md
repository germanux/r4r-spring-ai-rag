# Riansares 4R — external technical sources verified November 2026

## Scope and status of sources

**Verified access: 13 November 2026.** These sources support cross-checking general practices; they are not contracts or accreditations of Riansares 4R and do not justify attributing work to it. Prioritise Spanish standards and requirements for work in Spain. Do not copy extensive content from external pages; R4R guides are originally written.

## EXT-CTE-HS — Technical Building Code, basic health and hygiene document (Spain)

URL: https://www.codigotecnico.org/pdf/Documentos/HS/DBHS.pdf

Use: general criteria on moisture, ventilation and health and hygiene; check version and applicability to each intervention. Jurisdiction: Spain.

## EXT-BOE-REBT — Low Voltage Electrotechnical Regulation (Spain)

URL: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2002/08/02/842/con

Use: cautious references regarding electrical installations. Confirm the current text and specific obligations. Jurisdiction: Spain.

## EXT-IDAE-REHAB — IDAE, practical energy guide for renovation

URL: https://www.idae.es/tecnologias/eficiencia-energetica/edificacion/aislamiento-en-edificacion/guia-practica-de-la-energia

Use: planning insulation and thermal-envelope interventions. Jurisdiction: Spain; public information guide.

## EXT-EPA-MOISTURE — US EPA, How do I get rid of mold?

URL: https://www.epa.gov/mold/how-do-i-get-rid-mold

Use: prioritising repair of water sources and avoiding covering damp surfaces again. Application: general technical guidance; **not a Spanish standard**.

## EXT-EPA-GUIDE — US EPA, A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture and Your Home

URL: https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home

Use: drying, indoor humidity, prevention and safety. Application: guidance; not a Spanish standard.

## EXT-NARI-FAQ — National Association of the Remodeling Industry (United States)

URL: https://remodelingdoneright.nari.org/plan-your-remodel/faqs-glossary

Use: comparison of proposals, scope, quality levels, contracting and quotations. US industry; does not accredit or represent R4R.

## EXT-NARI-BUDGET — NARI, Budgeting and financing tips (United States)

URL: https://remodelingdoneright.nari.org/plan-your-remodel/budgeting-financing-tips/

Use: contingencies, priorities, contracts and quotations. Stated percentages are indicative of the US industry.

## EXT-NAHB-LIVING — National Association of Home Builders (United States)

URL: https://www.nahb.org/Other/Consumer-Resources/How-to-Live-with-Your-Remodeling-Project

Use: living with renovation, dust, service interruptions, communication and planning. US industry.

## EXT-HSE-ASBESTOS — UK Health and Safety Executive, asbestos refurbishment surveys

URL: https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/arrange-asbestos-survey.htm

Use: identifying risks before intrusive work. Jurisdiction: United Kingdom; **does not replace Spanish asbestos regulations**.

## EXT-GOVUK-VENT — Ventilation Approved Document F (England)

URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/approved-document-f-2026

Use: foreign example of ventilation specifications. Jurisdiction: England; **does not determine compliance with the Spanish CTE**.

## EXT-EPA-LEAD — US EPA, Lead Renovation, Repair and Painting

URL: https://www.epa.gov/lead/lead-renovation-repair-and-painting-program

Use: precautions regarding contaminated dust from old paint. **The US rule for homes built before 1978 is not the Spanish rule**.
