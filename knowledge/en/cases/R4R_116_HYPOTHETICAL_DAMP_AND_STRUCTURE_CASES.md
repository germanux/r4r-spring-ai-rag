# Hypothetical cases — damp, rehabilitation and structures

## H-21 — Roof leaks after wind

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Stains appear after wind-driven rain.

**Checks:** Roof junctions, façade flashings and gutters.

**Possible scope:** Repair the verified point and monitor.

**Decision criterion:** Do not assume roof tiles are responsible.

**Evidence to retain:** Rainfall photos and location.

## H-22 — Cold floor with an odour

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Ground floor with a persistent damp smell.

**Checks:** Ventilation, water beneath the concrete floor base and salts.

**Possible scope:** Diagnosis before insulation or finishes.

**Decision criterion:** Do not encapsulate moisture without a drying plan.

**Evidence to retain:** Hygrometer readings and plans.

## H-23 — Stone wall with salts

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Render is detaching from an old wall.

**Checks:** Moisture, salts and mortar compatibility.

**Possible scope:** Conservation/stripping back and compatible materials.

**Decision criterion:** Do not prescribe a universal mortar.

**Evidence to retain:** Wall characterisation.

## H-24 — New crack when enlarging an opening

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** A crack appears above the opening after work.

**Checks:** Loads and continuity, technical assessment.

**Possible scope:** Stop risky work and consult a professional.

**Decision criterion:** Do not apply filler before assessment.

**Evidence to retain:** Photographs and assessment.

## H-25 — Mould after internal insulation

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Mould appears at a floor-structure junction.

**Checks:** Thermal bridge, ventilation and moisture.

**Possible scope:** Review continuity and hygrothermal conditions.

**Decision criterion:** Do not add more insulation without diagnosis.

**Evidence to retain:** Measurements and construction detail.

## H-26 — Patio leaks into a basement

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Stains below ground level during heavy rain.

**Checks:** Drainage, junctions and lateral water pressure.

**Possible scope:** Water management solution compatible with the ground.

**Decision criterion:** Waterproof paint does not replace drainage.

**Evidence to retain:** Tests and rainwater routes.

## H-27 — Window condensation

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Recurring droplets on glass in the morning.

**Checks:** Indoor humidity, ventilation and thermal bridges.

**Possible scope:** Adjust ventilation and envelope according to cause.

**Decision criterion:** Do not diagnose a roof leak.

**Evidence to retain:** Relative humidity and photograph.

## H-28 — Swollen plaster after an old leak

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The pipe was repaired but plaster remains soft.

**Checks:** Drying and material condition.

**Possible scope:** Remove unstable material and finish when appropriate.

**Decision criterion:** Do not paint without checking the substrate.

**Evidence to retain:** Drying follow-up.

## H-29 — Roof with wet insulation

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Moisture is discovered within roof layers.

**Checks:** Source and extent, safe access.

**Possible scope:** Repair watertightness and replace damaged layers according to assessment.

**Decision criterion:** Do not enclose wet insulation.

**Evidence to retain:** Opening-up and closing-up sequence.

## H-30 — House closed for years

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Damp smell and salts on walls.

**Checks:** Ventilation, rising damp, leaks and roof.

**Possible scope:** Diagnosis by area and rehabilitation by priority.

**Decision criterion:** Do not promise a ready-to-live-in home after painting.

**Evidence to retain:** Damage map and priorities.
