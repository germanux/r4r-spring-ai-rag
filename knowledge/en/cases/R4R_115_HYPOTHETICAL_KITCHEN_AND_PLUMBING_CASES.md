# Hypothetical cases — kitchens, leaks and renovation decisions

## K-21 — Leak near a dishwasher

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Water appears beneath a built-in appliance after every wash.

**Checks:** Check supply connection, hose, drainage, joints and safe removal options.

**Possible scope:** Specific repair and review of furniture damage.

**Decision criterion:** Do not replace flooring without identifying the source.

**Evidence to retain:** Photos during use, of the component and of testing.

## K-22 — Constantly leaking tap

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The tap drips even when closed.

**Checks:** Distinguish cartridge, flexible hoses and connections; assess general condition.

**Possible scope:** Tap repair or replacement after diagnosis.

**Decision criterion:** Do not quote for a complete renovation.

**Evidence to retain:** Component and shut-off test.

## K-23 — Kitchen needs new flooring

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Floor tiles are broken while a pipe is being repaired.

**Checks:** Base condition, moisture and flooring continuity.

**Possible scope:** Local repair or complete floor tiling according to actual damage.

**Decision criterion:** Do not lay flooring over a damp base.

**Evidence to retain:** Damage plan and measurements.

## K-24 — Relocating a sink

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The aim is to place the sink beneath another window.

**Checks:** Drainage gradient, water supply and distances.

**Possible scope:** New routing if feasible and accessible for inspection.

**Decision criterion:** Do not decide solely on appearance.

**Evidence to retain:** Sketch and levels.

## K-25 — Detached finish behind the sink

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The wall sounds hollow and there is longstanding damp.

**Checks:** Check adhesion, moisture source and substrate.

**Possible scope:** Strip back, dry and apply suitable wall tiling.

**Decision criterion:** Do not bond onto loose tiles.

**Evidence to retain:** Photos of each layer.

## K-26 — Partial renovation retaining cabinets

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The client has a limited budget and wants pipe repairs.

**Checks:** Access and potential to salvage cabinets.

**Possible scope:** Plumbing replacement and finishing without replacing everything.

**Decision criterion:** Separate essential/optional expenses.

**Evidence to retain:** Inventory of retained cabinets.

## K-27 — Appliances and insufficient power

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** A hob, oven and dishwasher are added.

**Checks:** Installation characteristics and power/circuits.

**Possible scope:** Electrical adaptation by an authorised professional if appropriate.

**Decision criterion:** Do not use a single multi-socket adaptor as the solution.

**Evidence to retain:** Installation report.

## K-28 — Unventilated utility room

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Laundry area with odours and condensation.

**Checks:** Connections, leaks, extraction and ambient humidity.

**Possible scope:** Water and ventilation solution followed by finishes.

**Decision criterion:** Do not recommend only a dehumidifier.

**Evidence to retain:** Comparative measurements.

## K-29 — Water-swollen cabinet

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Panels beneath the sink are deformed.

**Checks:** Leak repair and material condition.

**Possible scope:** Dry and repair or replace affected panels.

**Decision criterion:** Do not assure that original dimensions will return.

**Evidence to retain:** Photos and replacement scope.

## K-30 — Cracked worktop

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The worktop has cracked after base movement.

**Checks:** Supports, joints and movement source.

**Possible scope:** Specialist repair or replacement according to material and risk.

**Decision criterion:** Do not seal a structural crack without diagnosis.

**Evidence to retain:** Views of supports and damage.

## K-31 — Renovation without removing tiles

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The aim is a new appearance with minimal rubble.

**Checks:** Adhesion, damp, thicknesses and finishing details.

**Possible scope:** Compatible solution respecting services.

**Decision criterion:** Do not hide leaks with decorative finishes.

**Evidence to retain:** System data sheet and tests.

## K-32 — Two homes affected

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** Replacing a kitchen drainage stack affects the floor below.

**Checks:** Ownership and access between homes.

**Possible scope:** Coordinate repair and damage reinstatement.

**Decision criterion:** Do not assign responsibility without a report.

**Evidence to retain:** Records and routing.
