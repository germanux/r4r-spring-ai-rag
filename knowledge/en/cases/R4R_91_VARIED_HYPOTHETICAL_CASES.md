# Riansares 4R — hypothetical consultation cases to broaden coverage

## Provenance notice for ALL cases

**All cases C-01 to C-12 are hypothetical and fictional. They do not represent work completed, clients served, quotations issued or results achieved by Riansares 4R.** Their purpose is to help explain common renovation questions and decisions. Every solution requires verification of the real case.

## C-01 — Kitchen with a leak and damaged cabinets (hypothetical)

Question: “A pipe has broken behind the kitchen cabinets; must I replace them all?”

Guidance: Shut off the water supply, assess safety, repair and verify the pipe, inspect moisture and cabinet condition. Replace only damaged elements or those the owner chooses to renew; there is no general technical obligation to replace every cabinet. Document damage for insurance where appropriate.

## C-02 — Kitchen renovation and tiles (hypothetical)

Question: “I have old leaks, want to renovate and then replace tiles. In what order?”

Guidance: Leak diagnosis; define water, drainage and electricity routes; necessary repair or renewal; watertightness tests; substrate repair and preparation; wall tiling and cabinet installation. Assess an itemised quotation without inventing floor area.

## C-03 — Kitchen with new appliances (hypothetical)

Question: “If I renovate the plumbing, can I install a new oven and dishwasher?”

Guidance: Yes, as an optional part of renovation, checking dimensions, water connections, drainage, ventilation and appropriate electrical circuits. Replacing pipes does not make appliance replacement compulsory.

## C-04 — Windowless bathroom with recurring mould (hypothetical)

Question: “I clean mould from the ceiling, but it returns. Should I repaint?”

Guidance: Diagnose moisture and ventilation; repair any leak; review extractor and substrate. Cleaning and painting without moisture control may fail. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/mold/how-do-i-get-rid-mold).

## C-05 — Narrow bathroom with toilet relocation (hypothetical)

Question: “Can I turn the toilet to gain space?”

Guidance: Measure distances, drain, gradient, stack, floor level and access; establish feasibility before promising a change. Building work and drainage alterations may be required.

## C-06 — Old wall with salt deposits (hypothetical)

Question: “Should I put lime mortar on a wall with salts?”

Guidance: Locate the moisture source, clean and prepare a compatible substrate, assess salts and drying before choosing the finish. Lime mortar does not eliminate water entry.

## C-07 — Patio sloping towards a door (hypothetical)

Question: “Water enters when it rains after laying tiles. What should I check?”

Guidance: Levels, gradient, drain, threshold, gutters and junctions. Avoid solutions that raise water even further beside the door.

## C-08 — Office work during business hours (hypothetical)

Question: “Can we renovate the toilets without stopping office activity?”

Guidance: Plan phases, service interruptions, isolated zones, user safety and alternative toilets; feasibility depends on occupancy and technical requirements.

## C-09 — Commercial premises with a partition to remove (hypothetical)

Question: “I want to remove a wall to enlarge my shop.”

Guidance: First assess stability, services, escape routes, licensing and accessibility. Do not determine load-bearing status from a verbal description.

## C-10 — Pool water leak (hypothetical)

Question: “The pool loses water; is painting the shell enough?”

Guidance: No; first determine whether loss comes from evaporation, hydraulic systems, joints or shell lining. Do not promise that paint repairs leaks.

## C-11 — Home renovation with occupants (hypothetical)

Question: “We must live in the flat while renovating the kitchen and bathroom.”

Guidance: Agree phases, interruptions, access, dust, temporary storage and available toilets. Work is more complex in an occupied property. [NAHB](https://www.nahb.org/Other/Consumer-Resources/How-to-Live-with-Your-Remodeling-Project).

## C-12 — Quotation priced per square metre (hypothetical)

Question: “How much does renovating 80 m² cost, with no other information?”

Guidance: Do not provide a fixed figure; ask about layout, services condition, quality levels, access, location and scope. If using a documented example, identify its date and state that it is not a current rate.
