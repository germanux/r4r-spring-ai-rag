# Site safety — permits, hazardous materials and waste

## Does replacing tiles require a licence?

The procedure depends on the municipality and scope, particularly if services or structural elements are affected. Verify local procedures before starting; there is no single answer for all Spain.

## What should be done before demolishing a partition?

Check structural function or concealed services, protection, responsibilities and permits. An apparently thin wall must not be demolished without the necessary assessment.

## Who should modify an electrical installation?

Work requiring authorisation must be performed by authorised professionals and companies under applicable Spanish regulations. Do not recommend bypassing protective devices or tampering with electrical panels.

## What if asbestos is suspected?

Do not cut, sand, drill or handle suspected material. Request specialist assessment and apply Spanish identification, removal and management requirements. Foreign guides provide guidance rather than replacing local regulations.

## How is construction rubble managed?

Separate and deliver waste according to its type, quantity and applicable management rules. Keep waste-management documents where required. Requirements for minor works may differ from those for major works.

## Why must roof work not be carried out without protection?

There is a fall risk, so work must be planned with competent personnel and suitable equipment. A small repair does not eliminate the hazard.

## Can paint or adhesives be poured down drains?

No. Leftover materials must be managed according to their characteristics and applicable regulations, rather than improvised disposal into drainage networks.

## What should be checked when working in open premises?

Access control, signage, dust, noise, energised services, escape routes and public protection. If safe segregation is impossible, the affected area must close.

## Is a technical design required to move a wall?

It depends on its function, scope and regulations. Relocation affecting structure, safety or use may require a design; quotation terminology must not determine this.

## Why keep photos of concealed services?

They support maintenance, future drilling, incident resolution and traceability of completed work. They do not replace mandatory regulatory documentation.

## Case S-11 — Pipe leaking onto an electrical panel

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** A leak approaches an electrical panel.

**Checks:** Keep people away, assess safe isolation and assistance.

**Possible scope:** Coordinate plumbing and an electrician.

**Decision criterion:** Do not instruct anyone to touch a wet panel.

**Evidence to retain:** Incident report and checks.

## Case S-12 — Renovation in an old building

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** A suspicious board is discovered during removal work.

**Checks:** Do not handle it; obtain professional identification and check requirements.

**Possible scope:** Specialist treatment before continuing.

**Decision criterion:** Do not conceal hazardous material or transport it with ordinary rubble.

**Evidence to retain:** Assessment and documented authorised management.
