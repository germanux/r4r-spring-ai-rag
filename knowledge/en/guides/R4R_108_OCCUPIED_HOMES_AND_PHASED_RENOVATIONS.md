# Work in occupied homes — phases, safety and coordination

## Can I live at home during renovation?

It depends on scope, available services, dust, noise, access and risks. If work affects the only bathroom or kitchen, a temporary solution or vacating certain areas may be necessary. Do not promise habitability without assessing phases.

## How is a kitchen renovation planned without an alternative kitchen?

Agree interruption dates, safe food and equipment storage, waste removal and personnel access. A realistic schedule indicates periods without water, electricity or a sink.

## Can a bathroom be renovated without losing all services?

If there are several bathrooms or phased work is possible, some services may remain. With a single toilet, assess a temporary solution; do not assure everyday use if networks are disconnected.

## How are furniture and corridors protected?

Define storage areas, material routes, surface-compatible protection, dust enclosures and cleaning. Protection does not replace ventilation and occupational safety measures.

## What should neighbours or the owners’ association be told?

Indicative schedule, noisy work, lift or communal-area use, material removal and shared service interruptions where applicable. Follow permits and association rules.

## How are keys and access managed?

Agree responsible persons, authorisations, hours and handover records. Do not share access codes with unnecessary third parties; establish an incident communication channel.

## What happens if work reveals asbestos or another hazardous material?

Stop unplanned handling, isolate the area and consult qualified personnel under applicable Spanish regulations. The British HSE guide is cited solely as a good-practice reference, not Spanish law.

## When can an area be closed up after a leak?

When the cause has been repaired, services tested and substrates are suitable for finishes. Quickly enclosing a damp wall may prevent monitoring.

## Is overlapping all trades a good idea?

Only when tasks are compatible in safety, access and sequence. Poorly coordinated simultaneous work can interfere with checks and increase rework.

## How is phased handover documented?

Partial handover records, completed items, outstanding work, tests, photos and safe use of handed-over areas. Distinguish aesthetic completion from acceptance of services.

**Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** There is no other toilet in the home.

**Checks:** Unavailability period and alternatives.

**Possible scope:** Temporary arrangements and accommodation option if necessary.

**Decision criterion:** Do not minimise service interruptions.

**Evidence to retain:** Schedule and validation with occupants.
