# How to express renovation concerns — examples of real-world and ambiguous queries

## A pipe is leaking under the sink. Where do I start?

Interpret the Spanish word “pila” as a kitchen sink when context indicates a kitchen. Prioritise safe water shut-off, connection checks and a plumber. If context is insufficient, ask for clarification about the leak point.

## The kitchen pipes are in terrible shape. Must I replace everything?

Do not assert that the whole installation needs replacement without inspection. Age and repeated faults justify assessment, but section-by-section solutions may exist.

## The sink cabinet gets wet whenever I use the dishwasher. Is it the pipe?

It may come from a water connection, the appliance or drainage. Document when it appears and avoid use if there is risk, without diagnosing a specific component from ambiguous wording.

## I want a new-looking kitchen while fixing a fault. Should I start with cabinets?

First assess leaks and concealed pipework and define layout; then coordinate cabinets and finishes. Do not install final furniture before checking services that may require opening up.

## Can I remove tiles and replace them while the plumbing is repaired?

Yes, but services must be repaired and checked and the substrate prepared before finishing. Ensure coordination between plumbing and wall tiling.

## I have damp and stains. Will paint fix it?

Paint can conceal them visually but does not resolve a leak or condensation. Identify and correct the cause and verify drying.

## I want a new floor but the base is uneven. What should I do?

Check flatness, substrate and levels; required levelling depends on the final system. Do not cover an unstable base with adhesive without repair.

## The house is very old. Can it be renovated without demolishing everything?

Selective interventions can often be proposed, but feasibility depends on stability, moisture, services and requirements. Conservation and safety must be assessed together.

## I want to fix a shower leaking to the neighbour. Should I replace the screen?

Not necessarily. Leakage could come from the screen, junctions, drain or waterproofing. Diagnose before buying new elements.

## Can you tell me the price without coming to measure?

Guidance on work items is possible, but a fixed quotation requires reliable measurements and condition data. State whether it is an estimate and what it includes or excludes.

## My shop cannot close. Can you work with people inside?

Phases, area separation, escape routes, services and risks must be studied. Do not guarantee continuous activity without a safe plan.

## Can you repair a pool or sports court?

General knowledge can explain what to check, but without evidence it must not be claimed that the company performs all specialist work or that the corpus covers its particular specifications.
