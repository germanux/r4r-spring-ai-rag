# Kitchens — scope, materials and trade coordination

## Can I replace wall tiles even if only one pipe is broken?

Yes. It is an optional improvement compatible with the repair, provided the fault is addressed first and the substrates and pipe routes are checked. Itemize plumbing, removal, surface preparation, tiling and finishing separately in the quotation.

## Can I keep the cabinets while the plumbing is replaced?

That depends on access to the affected pipe, the cabinets' condition, water damage and whether they can be removed and reinstalled. Photograph them before dismantling, and verify that appliances retain adequate ventilation and accessible connections.

## What if I also want to replace cabinets and appliances?

This can be part of the project, but cabinet and appliance dimensions must be coordinated with water supplies, drainage, electrical circuits and ventilation. Replacing cabinets does not necessarily mean relocating all services.

## What needs to be decided before installing a countertop?

Confirm the sink, hob and appliance positions; openings and clearances; junctions with walls; taps; power outlets; finishing details and maintenance access. Check final measurements after wall surfaces have been prepared.

## What precautions apply when laying new tiles over existing ones?

Check adhesion, flatness, moisture, permissible loads, junction details and compatibility of the adhesive system. Repair or remove loose tiles and deteriorated substrate first. Do not promise that tiling over existing finishes is always suitable.

## Can I change the layout and add an island?

Only after measuring circulation routes, doors, storage, services, structural considerations and ventilation. An island may require routing services under the floor and alter passage widths. Aesthetic preference alone is not enough to justify it.

## When should kitchen sockets be reviewed?

Before closing service chases and fitting cabinets. Check intended uses, appliance positions, protective devices and circuits against applicable regulations. Electrical modifications must be carried out by appropriately qualified professionals where required.

## Why is kitchen ventilation important?

Assess extraction, replacement air, ventilation routes and equipment needs under applicable requirements. New cabinets alone do not resolve ventilation or fumes extraction.

## Why cannot an exact price be given from a photograph?

Measurements, concealed-service condition, material quality, access, removal, waste disposal and location are unknown. A price example for a 7 m² kitchen cannot be applied to another kitchen without adequate information.

## Should plumbing, electrical work or tiling come first?

Define the layout and services before the finishes. The specific sequence depends on scope and trade coordination. Generally, services are installed and tested before walls are closed and final finishes are laid.

## Case C-01 — Older kitchen with insufficient sockets

**Hypothetical evaluation case, not work performed or a service promised by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The owner wants to keep the kitchen but add several appliances.

**Checks:** Inventory appliances, circuits and actual connection positions.

**Possible scope:** Documented electrical adaptation and finishes compatible with existing cabinets.

**Decision criterion:** Do not recommend improvised chains of multi-plug adaptors or extensions.

**Evidence to retain:** Outlet plan, certification if applicable and photographs.

## Case C-02 — Renovation in an occupied dwelling

**Hypothetical evaluation case, not work performed or a service promised by Riansares 4R.**

**Situation:** The kitchen is the household's only food-preparation area.

**Checks:** Planned water and power outages, dust, access and cabinet removal.

**Possible scope:** Viable work phases and a temporary domestic alternative.

**Decision criterion:** Do not promise continued use when the necessary services will be unavailable.

**Evidence to retain:** Schedule, safety measures and handover record.
