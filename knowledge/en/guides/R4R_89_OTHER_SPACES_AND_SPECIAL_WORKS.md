# Riansares 4R — other works: pools, industrial buildings, terraces and facilities

## Can a leaking swimming pool be repaired?

First distinguish evaporation from leakage through the pool shell, joints, pipes, skimmers or fittings. Watertightness diagnosis and assessment of material and system compatibility are required; painting over a crack can hide the symptom. Do not quote without inspection or claim that Riansares 4R routinely provides this service.

## What should be considered before renovating sports centre changing rooms?

Intensive use, ventilation, moisture, gradients, drainage, suitable slip-resistant finishes, accessibility, services and maintenance, as well as phased closures. Specific public-use and safety requirements must be verified. **This is not a confirmed service in Riansares 4R’s operating catalogue.**

## What should be checked before renovating hospitality premises?

Use and occupancy, extraction, drainage, fire safety, accessibility, electricity, hygiene and possible gas installations. Specific regulations and specialist professionals are required. This corpus provides general guidance, not an offer of hospitality renovation work.

## How should an industrial building renovation be approached?

First define activity, imposed loads, access, structure, roof, electrical installation, fire protection, occupational safety and permits. Do not recommend structural solutions or assume qualification for large projects.

## What should be checked when waterproofing a terrace above homes?

Confirm the source of water ingress, drainage, gradients, junctions with parapets, doors and downpipes, layer compatibility and safe execution. A complete system design and work on communal elements may be needed; consult professionals and the owners’ association.
