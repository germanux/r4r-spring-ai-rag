# Riansares 4R — additional structured data

## Usage rule

These amounts are not Riansares 4R rates or binding offers. They are dated references from market guides and a technical work item. Before quoting, review measurements, condition, access, quality levels, waste, licences, VAT, margin and risks.

## Non-binding example quotations

### B-001 — Complete renovation of an 80 m2 home

- **Low range:** 32,000 EUR
- **Base:** 42,000 EUR
- **High range:** 52,000 EUR
- **Documentary basis:** P-001, SRC-HAB-VIVIENDA
- **Assumptions:** mid-range quality, no structural work, moderate layout changes, normal access
- **Exclusions:** VAT, technical design where required, fees, asbestos, structural damage
- **Calculation:** 80 m2 multiplied by the 400–650 EUR/m2 range; the baseline approximates the published example starting at 40.000 EUR.

### B-002 — 120 m2 old house without structural strengthening

- **Low range:** 42,000 EUR
- **Base:** 50,000 EUR

- **Documentary basis:** P-002, SRC-HAB-ANTIGUA
- **Assumptions:** no structural work, no extensive layout changes, mid-range quality
- **Exclusions:** complete roof, foundations, structural damp, technical design and fees
- **Calculation:** 120 m2 at 350–450 EUR/m2. The source publishes an itemised 41.900 EUR example.

### B-003 — 120 m2 old house with structural work and layout changes

## Caution criteria

- A price per m² supports comparison, not quotations without measurements.
- The CYPE work item describes a specific configuration and does not automatically include all business costs.
- Market guides aggregate heterogeneous projects.
- References must retain their date and source.
- VAT and licences are verified for each transaction.
