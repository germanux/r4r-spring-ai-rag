Riansares 4R · Evidence-backed AI for renovations

An experimental assistant connected to the Riansares 4R knowledge base. It focuses on renovations, plumbing, waterproofing, masonry, materials, measurements and preliminary cost estimates. It provides traceable sources or abstains when the available documentation does not support an answer.

What you can ask
  • Initial assessment: what information is needed to evaluate a renovation project.
  • Technical concepts: differences between concrete subfloors, floor finishes, mortars and construction systems.
  • Waterproofing and repairs: damp, roofs, leaks and finishes.
  • Preliminary estimates: scope, line items and measurement criteria.
Important: this is not a general-purpose chatbot. The demo is designed for questions related to Riansares 4R's work.
Scope

Real-world construction questions

FAQs, technical criteria, estimates, real cases and Riansares 4R documentation.

Output

Answer + sources

Retrieves relevant evidence, generates a document-grounded answer and cites the source documents and sections.

Limitation

No unsupported answers

If a question is out of scope or lacks sufficient supporting evidence, the assistant abstains.

Retrieval

The question is converted into an embedding and compared with document chunks about renovations, materials and estimates.

Grounding

Retrieved passages are labeled and supplied to the model as verifiable context for its answer.

Abstention

If the question cannot be answered from the Riansares 4R documentation, the system abstains.

Deployment

The API and PostgreSQL run in Docker; local model inference runs through Ollama and is connected to the demo.
