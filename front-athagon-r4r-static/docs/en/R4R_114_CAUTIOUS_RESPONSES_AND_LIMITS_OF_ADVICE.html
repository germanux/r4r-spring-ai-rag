Visitor guidance — what a renovation assistant can answer

What does initial guidance mean?

An initial explanation based on available documents. It does not replace a site visit, measurement, design, report by a competent technical professional or quotations with confirmed scope.

Why can some questions not be answered?

Documentation may not cover a specific material, installation, area or requirement. Identifying missing information is preferable to inventing prices, brands, schedules or conclusions.

Is a photograph enough to establish the cost?

No. A photograph provides context, but measurements, substrates, access, finish level, waste and possible concealed services are missing. Estimation requires scope and market data.

Can it be guaranteed that a wall is not load-bearing?

Not without competent analysis. The assistant can explain what to verify but cannot certify structural stability.

Is maintenance advice an instruction to carry out work?

No. Documentary explanation must distinguish safety recommendations, technical inspection and contractual decisions. Users need professional confirmation for risky interventions.

What does it mean when an answer includes sources?

It identifies documentary excerpts used as evidence. A citation does not automatically guarantee that every detail is supported; visitors must be able to check the connection.

Should an example price be cited as the user’s actual cost?

No. If a document contains the example “7 m² kitchen”, that figure must not be applied to a kitchen of unknown size unless unequivocally identified as an inapplicable example.

What if a query combines a leak and aesthetic renovation?

Address the functional or safety problem first, then optional improvements. State that changing tiles, cabinets or appliances is not an obligatory consequence of repairing pipes.

What if a source only explains a hypothetical case?

Explain that it is an illustrative example. Do not present it as a completed project, real testimonial or company experience.

What if the user asks about a specific law?

Refer to a verifiable regulatory source for the stated jurisdiction and explain that application depends on the work. Do not present US or British requirements as Spanish law.
