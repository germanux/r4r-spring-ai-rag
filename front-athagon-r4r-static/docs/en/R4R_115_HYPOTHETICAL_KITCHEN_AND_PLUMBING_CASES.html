Hypothetical cases — kitchens, leaks and renovation decisions

K-21 — Leak near a dishwasher

Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.

Situation: Water appears beneath a built-in appliance after every wash.

Checks: Check supply connection, hose, drainage, joints and safe removal options.

Possible scope: Specific repair and review of furniture damage.

Decision criterion: Do not replace flooring without identifying the source.

Evidence to retain: Photos during use, of the component and of testing.

K-22 — Constantly leaking tap

Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.

Situation: The tap drips even when closed.

Checks: Distinguish cartridge, flexible hoses and connections; assess general condition.

Possible scope: Tap repair or replacement after diagnosis.

Decision criterion: Do not quote for a complete renovation.

Evidence to retain: Component and shut-off test.

K-23 — Kitchen needs new flooring

Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.

Situation: Floor tiles are broken while a pipe is being repaired.

Checks: Base condition, moisture and flooring continuity.

Possible scope: Local repair or complete floor tiling according to actual damage.

Decision criterion: Do not lay flooring over a damp base.

Evidence to retain: Damage plan and measurements.

K-24 — Relocating a sink

Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.

Situation: The aim is to place the sink beneath another window.

Checks: Drainage gradient, water supply and distances.

Possible scope: New routing if feasible and accessible for inspection.

Decision criterion: Do not decide solely on appearance.

Evidence to retain: Sketch and levels.

K-25 — Detached finish behind the sink

Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.

Situation: The wall sounds hollow and there is longstanding damp.

Checks: Check adhesion, moisture source and substrate.

Possible scope: Strip back, dry and apply suitable wall tiling.

Decision criterion: Do not bond onto loose tiles.

Evidence to retain: Photos of each layer.

Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.

Situation: The client has a limited budget and wants pipe repairs.
