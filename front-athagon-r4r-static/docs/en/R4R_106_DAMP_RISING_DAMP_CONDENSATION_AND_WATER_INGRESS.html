Damp — identifying the source and repair decisions

How is condensation distinguished from water ingress?

Condensation is usually associated with cold surfaces, indoor humidity and insufficient ventilation; water ingress with water entering from the roof, façade or services. They can coexist. Location, timing, rainfall and measurements help, but a single photo does not complete the diagnosis.

Is moisture rising up a wall always capillary action?

No. Leaks in buried pipework, hygroscopic salts, lateral water ingress or localised condensation may be involved. Confirm the source before recommending chemical barriers or mortars.

Does painting over a yellow stain help?

Painting may improve appearance if the cause has already been repaired and the substrate is dry. If leakage or condensation persists, staining and deterioration will probably return.

Why is there mould behind a wardrobe?

Air may circulate poorly over a cold wall, allowing moisture to condense. Concealed water ingress is also possible. Move the furniture away, ventilate and assess the source before covering the wall.

Can a damp wall be concealed with plasterboard?

An active problem should not be enclosed without diagnosis; this could encourage hidden damage. If used after repair, the system must accommodate residual moisture, ventilation and substrate compatibility.

What information does a moisture meter provide?

It can indicate differences between areas or drying progress. Readings depend on material, salts and method; they do not replace identifying the water source or justify a single cause.

When should salt-affected finishes be removed?

When finishes lose adhesion or salts indicate persistent water, identify the cause and assess stripping back and compatible materials. Do not promise that breathable mortar alone stops water ingress.

What if mould appears after replacing windows?

The balance between air infiltration and ventilation may have changed. Assess ventilation, thermal bridges and use, without automatically concluding that the windows are defective.

Which moisture problems take priority in a renovation?

First active water entry and risks to people or services; then drying, material diagnosis and finishes. Do not apply permanent finishes before resolving the cause.

How can resolution of damp be demonstrated?

Record the identified cause, implemented solution, verification tests and follow-up after rain/use where appropriate. A photo taken immediately after painting does not by itself demonstrate long-term effectiveness.

Case H-12 — Winter mould

Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.

Situation: Cold wall with mould in a bedroom corner.

Checks: Ambient humidity, ventilation, thermal bridge and possible leaks.

Possible scope: Moisture control and intervention compatible with the source.

Decision criterion: Do not assert rising damp without evidence.

Evidence to retain: Temperatures/humidity and seasonal photos.
