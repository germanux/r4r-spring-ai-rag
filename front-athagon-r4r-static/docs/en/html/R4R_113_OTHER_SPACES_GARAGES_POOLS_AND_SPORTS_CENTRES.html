Other spaces — garages, pools, sports areas and industrial buildings

Can a damp garage be renovated like a room?

Not without examining ventilation, water, fire safety, compatibility with use and possible administrative requirements. Decorative flooring does not make a garage habitable.

What should be checked before replacing a sports centre floor?

Use, friction, impacts, flatness, substrate moisture, maintenance, safety and applicable sports regulations. Do not equate sports-court flooring with domestic ceramic tiles.

How should repair of a leaking pool begin?

Determine whether evaporation, pipes, the pool shell or connections are responsible through appropriate checks. Do not assert defective waterproofing without locating the source.

Can public changing rooms be renovated without closing them?

Only with phases compatible with hygiene, access, escape routes and users. Water and wet surfaces require particular attention to slipping and services.

Is a ramp suitable for any public entrance?

No. Gradients, widths, landings, protection and compliance with applicable accessibility regulations must be verified. Drawing a ramp does not prove feasibility.

Can an industrial building be painted without checking the substrate?

Exposure, corrosion, contamination, moisture and system compatibility must be known. The chosen finish varies with material and use.

What should be checked in a plant room?

Access, ventilation, maintenance, electrical safety, water, fire protection and documentation. Appearance is secondary to operation and safety.

How is a building with sporting activities renovated?

Coordinate hours, safety zones, circulation and cleaning without interfering with users. Planning differs from that for an empty home.

Are the same tiles suitable for changing rooms and living rooms?

Not necessarily; required water resistance, slip resistance, cleaning and traffic performance may differ. Choose using technical data sheets and intended use.

Can the RAG method be adapted to these facilities?

Document retrieval architecture can answer questions about any domain with suitable sources, but a corpus mainly covering residential renovations does not demonstrate comprehensive technical competence in sports facilities, pools or industry.

Hypothetical assessment case, not completed work or a service committed to by Riansares 4R.

Situation: Slips are reported when leaving showers.

Checks: Current material, gradients, cleaning and performance.

Possible scope: Repair suited to use and safe circulation.

Decision criterion: Surface treatment alone is insufficient without testing.
