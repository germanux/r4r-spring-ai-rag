Riansares 4R — quotations, measurements, extras and scope changes

What do I need to request a renovation quotation?

Photos, location, approximate area, problem, expected result, layout, desired materials, access, current use, deadlines and priorities. This information supports initial guidance but does not replace a visit, measurements or diagnosis of concealed services. For kitchens and bathrooms, state whether water supply and drainage positions will be retained.

Why can two renovation quotations differ greatly?

They may include different scope, materials, substrate preparation, labour, waste removal, administrative procedures, access, warranties, risks and contingencies. Compare item by item and clarify exclusions. NARI recommends comparing bids with the same scope and quality level.

How is hidden damage discovered when opening a wall priced?

With photos, a description of findings, a technical proposal, additional measurements and prior approval of the change before carrying out the affected work, except urgent protection or safety measures that must be agreed. An initial quotation should clarify which checks were impossible before opening up.

Why are prices not based solely on square metres?

The m² do not describe concealed leaks, concrete bases, drains, out-of-plumb surfaces, material transport, services, finishes, furniture removal, licences or equipment. A €/m² value may be used as a retrospective indicator, rather than a generic fixed price.

Can renovations be carried out in phases?

Yes. Prioritise safety, leaks and services; then healthy conditions, waterproofing and substrates; finally finishes, decoration and furniture. Check that separating phases does not require repeated demolition or compromise technical solutions.

Should money be set aside for unforeseen work?

It is prudent to allow a contingency proportionate to the property’s condition and uncertainties. As US industry guidance, NARI mentions a reserve of 10–20 %; this is neither a rate nor a Spanish obligation, and each case must be estimated individually.

How should a partial repair be compared with a complete renovation?

Define the problem, service life of retained elements, access cost, duplicated work and expected usefulness. Compare two explicit scopes: repair the fault and reinstate affected areas; or take the opportunity to change layout, services and finishes. Do not assume the larger option is always preferable.

When can a kitchen or bathroom price be provided?

After establishing actual area, materials, scope and preparatory work. Example quotations in historical Riansares documents are conditional references; they must not be confused with a current offer or used in questions where no price was requested.

Hypothetical case PR-01 — Client asks to reduce a quotation

Fictional example. Rather than retaining scope and reducing concealed quality, propose keeping sound cabinets and replacing only deteriorated finishes and services, or scheduling separately priced phases. Any change requires reviewing work items and exclusions.
