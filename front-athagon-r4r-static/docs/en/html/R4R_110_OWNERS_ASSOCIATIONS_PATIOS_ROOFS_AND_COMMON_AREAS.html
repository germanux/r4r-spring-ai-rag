Owners’ associations — repairs and work on communal elements

Does a roof leak affect only one flat?

It may cause local damage while originating in a communal element. Identify location, ownership and responsibility using documentation and property regulations; do not assign blame without inspection.

Can a communal patio be repaired in sections?

Sometimes, if the system maintains continuity of waterproofing, gradients and drains. Disconnected repairs may fail at junctions between old and new areas.

What should be checked before work in an entrance hall?

Accessibility, routes, steps, gradients, escape routes, electrical installation and protection of pedestrian traffic. The scope may require a design or specific permits.

What happens if a communal downpipe is broken?

Coordinate with management and affected owners, contain damage and plan interruption and access. Responsibilities and permits depend on whether the section is private or communal.

Is painting a damp communal façade feasible?

Only after assessing finish condition, water entry and coating compatibility. Painting does not correct defective roof flashing or lateral water ingress.

Can work block the lift?

This should not happen without coordination and an alternative compatible with accessibility and safety. Inform users of restrictions and protect communal property.

Who approves changes in communal areas?

It depends on the ownership regime, association resolutions and applicable regulations. The assistant should recommend consulting management or professional advisers, rather than specifying voting majorities without context.

How is damage in neighbouring homes documented?

Dated photographs, location, progress, visits and communications. Distinguish confirmed damage from an as-yet unproven cause.

What should be done about falling material over a public street?

Prioritise cordoning off and preventing exposure, notify the relevant party and obtain a competent inspection. Do not start by quoting decorative painting.

How should an owners’ association quotation be compared?

Clear measurements, communal-area scope, access equipment, resident protection, waste, schedules, warranties and exclusions. The final total alone is insufficient to compare solutions.
