Technical sources — cautious application of Spanish, British and US guidance

Spain — CTE Basic Document HS

CTE DB HS addresses health and hygiene requirements, including protection against moisture, ventilation and water drainage. Specific applicability depends on use, intervention and the current text. Primary reference: https://www.codigotecnico.org/DocumentosCTE/Salubridad.html

Spain — CTE Basic Document SUA

CTE DB SUA covers safety in use and accessibility; it includes supporting documentation on slip resistance and adaptation in existing buildings. Each renovation must check applicable requirements. Primary reference: https://www.codigotecnico.org/DocumentosCTE/SeguridadUtilizacionAccesibilidad

Spain — CTE Basic Document HE

CTE DB HE sets out energy-saving requirements, including the envelope. Work on existing buildings requires analysis of scope and applicable rules. Primary reference: https://www.codigotecnico.org/DocumentosCTE/AhorroEnergia

Spain — BOE: construction and demolition waste

Royal Decree 105/2008 regulates construction and demolition waste, distinguishing minor works and management obligations by type. Consult the consolidated text and regional/local regulations. https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2008/02/01/105/con

Spain — IDAE: insulation and renovation

IDAE publishes guides on work on façades, roofs, floors and windows. Its examples provide energy guidance, rather than guaranteeing specific savings for a home. https://www.idae.es/tecnologias/eficiencia-energetica/edificacion/aislamiento-en-edificacion/guia-practica-de-la-energia

United States — EPA: moisture and mould

EPA explains that controlling moisture and repairing leaks underpin mould prevention and recommends rapid drying after water entry. This is US health guidance; it does not replace Spanish regulations. https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home

United Kingdom — HSE: asbestos assessment

HSE distinguishes management surveys from refurbishment/demolition surveys that identify concealed materials before work. These rules apply to the United Kingdom; Spain’s specific national regulations apply in Spain. https://www.hse.gov.uk/asbestos/duty/arrange-asbestos-survey.htm

United Kingdom — HSE: work at height

HSE stresses planning, risk assessment and suitable equipment for work at height. It is used here as international good-practice guidance, never as a transfer of British obligations to Spain. https://www.hse.gov.uk/Construction/faq-height.htm

Source hierarchy criterion

For questions about permits, regulations or safety in Spain, prioritise BOE, CTE, regional/municipal regulations and competent personnel. Foreign guides contextualise risks rather than establishing local obligations.

What to do when a source does not answer the query

Acknowledge limits: explain what must be checked without declaring schedules, prices, exact performance or certifications. A URL’s mere presence in a document does not substantiate the final answer.
