Riansares 4R — all frequently asked questions

Use and limits

Answers combine the canonical corpus with public information consulted on 27 July 2026. Prices are indicative. Procedures, VAT and warranties must be verified for the specific transaction and municipality.

Quotations

FAQ-001 — What information is needed for initial guidance?

When requesting a quotation, provide location, photos, approximate measurements, space use, problem, expected result and timeframe. Initial guidance does not replace a visit or measurements.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-002 — Why can a fixed price not be agreed from photos alone?

They do not confirm substrates, thicknesses, services, active damp, access, waste or hidden damage.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-003 — What should a quotation contain?

Scope, measurements, materials and quality levels, labour, waste, taxes, timeframes, payments, exclusions, extras and warranty.

Base: SRC-CM-REFORMAS

FAQ-004 — Is an itemised quotation better?

Yes. It enables comparison of quantities, quality levels and exclusions; a single total hides scope differences.

Base: SRC-CM-REFORMAS

FAQ-005 — Why is the price per square metre calculated at the end?

Each item is measured and priced first. The €/m² is a result indicator, not a universal rate.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-006 — What does an indicative price mean?

A dated, conditional reference; it must be confirmed through a visit, measurements, location, access, quality levels and taxes.

Base: SRC-HAB-VIVIENDA

FAQ-007 — How is hidden damage handled?

It is documented, diagnosed and priced before continuing the affected item, except agreed urgent safety action.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-008 — Can costs be reduced without degrading the solution?

Yes: reduce scope, separate optional items, retain sound elements, work in phases or explicitly change quality levels.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

Contracting

FAQ-009 — Should several quotations be requested?

Yes. Compare scope, quality levels, schedules, warranties and exclusions, rather than only the total.

Base: SRC-HAB-VIVIENDA

FAQ-010 — Is an advance payment normal?

It may be agreed for materials or a booking, always documented and tied to a quotation or contract.

Base: SRC-CM-REFORMAS

FAQ-011 — How are payments organised?

By verifiable milestones: limited advance, certified progress or work stages, and balance after handover review.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-012 — What happens with extra work?

It must be described, quoted and authorised, stating cost and schedule impacts.

Base: SRC-CM-REFORMAS

FAQ-013 — Can a material be changed during work?

Yes, documenting specification, quality, price, availability and schedule impact.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-014 — What documentation should the client keep?

Quotation, contract, payments, invoices, authorisations, changes, relevant data sheets, photos and handover record.

Base: SRC-CM-REFORMAS

Licences and VAT

FAQ-015 — Do I need a licence or responsible declaration?

It depends on the municipality and scope. In Tarancón, check the current procedure through its electronic office.

Base: SRC-TARANCON-LIC

FAQ-016 — Who handles the licence?

Agree in writing whether this is the client, technical professional or company, as well as who pays fees and prepares documentation.

Base: SRC-TARANCON-LIC

FAQ-017 — When should the owners’ association be consulted?

When structure, façade, services or communal elements are affected.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-018 — Do renovations attract 10% or 21% VAT?

The general rate is 21%. The 10% rate applies only to renovation or repair meeting all requirements.

Base: SRC-AEAT-IVA

FAQ-019 — What age requirement applies to reduced VAT?

Construction or rehabilitation must have been completed at least two years before work begins.

Base: SRC-AEAT-IVA

FAQ-020 — What happens if materials exceed 40%?

That reduced-rate requirement for renovation or repair is not met, and the general rate applies.

Base: SRC-AEAT-FAQ

FAQ-021 — Does boiler maintenance attract 10% VAT?

Not in itself. AEAT distinguishes maintenance from execution of building work; maintenance normally attracts 21%.

Base: SRC-AEAT-FAQ

FAQ-022 — Does pool repair attract 10% VAT?

AEAT states that pool repairs or renovations attract 21%.

Base: SRC-AEAT-FAQ

FAQ-023 — Can an insurance-funded repair attract 10% VAT?

It can, if the individual or owners’ association contracts and is liable for payment, and the other requirements are met.

Base: SRC-AEAT-FAQ

Damp

FAQ-024 — Why diagnose before choosing a product?

Rising damp, water ingress, plumbing leaks, condensation and salts require different solutions.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-025 — Does breathable mortar eliminate damp?

It does not eliminate active water entry; it improves compatibility and drying when the cause is controlled.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-026 — Can plasterboard be bonded to a damp wall?

It is unwise: it may hide developments and trap moisture. Wall linings must be designed with a compatible cavity and separation.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-027 — When should lime mortar be used?

On compatible old substrates, after checking strength, moisture, salts, thickness and finish.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-028 — What is a pilot test for?

To measure adhesion, consumption, time, thickness and appearance before covering the entire surface.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

Old houses

FAQ-029 — Why is selective demolition advisable?

It reduces waste, risks, damage to services and loss of still-sound elements.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-030 — What indicative cost applies to complete renovation of an old house?

The consulted guide gives 350–450 €/m² without structural work or layout changes; structural work or changes may raise this to 533–600 €/m² or more.

Base: SRC-HAB-ANTIGUA

Bathrooms

FAQ-031 — How much does renovating a bathroom cost?

Consulted examples range from 1.560 € for 2 m² to 4.860 € for a high-quality 6 m² bathroom; actual scope may exceed these figures.

Base: SRC-HAB-BANO

FAQ-032 — Does moving a toilet increase cost?

It may, because of the stack, gradient, diameter, height, inspection access and demolition work.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-033 — Is moisture-resistant board waterproof?

No. A shower needs a continuous waterproofing system and treated junctions.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-034 — Which toilet parts must not be confused?

The angle valve, flexible connector, fill valve, flush mechanism and push button perform different functions.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-035 — Repair or replace an old toilet?

It depends on ceramic condition, fixings, seals, spare parts, corrosion and the planned replacement timeframe.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

Kitchens

FAQ-036 — How much does renovating a 7 m² kitchen cost?

The consulted reference gives around 5.600–9.000 €, with about 6.000 € for mid-range quality.

Base: SRC-HAB-COCINA

FAQ-037 — How much can cabinets add?

In a small kitchen, they can add several thousand euros; separate them from building work and services.

Base: SRC-HAB-COCINA

FAQ-038 — Does opening the kitchen into the living room require assessment?

Yes. Identify whether the wall is a partition or load-bearing element and check permits, ventilation and services.

Base: SRC-HAB-COCINA

Floors

FAQ-039 — How much does installing laminate flooring cost?

The consulted reference gives around 25–30 €/m² including labour and a broad range of 20–65 €/m².

Base: SRC-HAB-LAMINADO

FAQ-040 — Can tiles be laid over tiles?

Yes, if the substrate is sound, clean, flat and well bonded; check levels, doors, moisture and hollow tiles.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-041 — What is the difference between a concrete floor base and a floor finish?

The concrete floor base is the structural or levelling base; the floor finish is the final flooring.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

Painting

FAQ-042 — How much does painting a flat cost?

The consulted guide publishes 10–12 €/m²; a 100 m² flat is around 1.300 € in white without exceptional preparation.

Base: SRC-HAB-PINTURA

FAQ-043 — Why paint the entire surface after a leak?

A patch usually leaves differences in shade and texture; first confirm the substrate is dry.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

Demolition

FAQ-044 — What does a technical wall-tile removal item include?

The consulted CYPE configuration includes manual removal, chipping off bedding material and manual loading; it gives 8,90 €/m² before adaptation to actual work.

Base: SRC-CYPE-DEMO-ALIC

Patios

FAQ-045 — Why does access change the price of a concrete floor base?

Manual mixing and transport increase working days, coordination and risk compared with direct discharge or pumping.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-046 — What should be checked before paving a patio?

Base, compaction, thickness, reinforcement, gradients, joints, inspection chambers, channels, junctions and access.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

Services

FAQ-047 — Should services be renewed before closing up finishes?

Yes, when aged or incompatible, to avoid reopening walls and floors.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-048 — Must an authorised professional be involved?

Yes, when regulations require it, particularly for electricity, gas, heating and cooling or certificates.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

Handover

FAQ-049 — What is checked before completing work?

Services, leaks, drains, seals, gradients, doors, finishing details, cleaning, damage and snagging list.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

Warranty

FAQ-050 — What warranty does renovation carry?

The service warranty must appear in the quotation, contract and invoice; components and materials retain their applicable statutory warranty.

Base: SRC-CM-GARANTIA

FAQ-051 — Does the warranty cover new water entry?

Not automatically. Distinguish workmanship defects from external causes, pre-existing substrates or lack of maintenance.

Base: BASE-CANONICA

FAQ-052 — How is an incident reported?

In writing, identifying the work item and symptoms, providing photos and allowing inspection before attributing a cause.

Base: SRC-CM-REFORMAS

Extended practical queries — November 2026

The following answers provide guidance. They do not establish completed work or replace professional diagnosis.

FAQ-053 — A kitchen pipe has broken. What should I do first?

For an active leak, shut off the accessible supply if safe, avoid wet electrical installations and request professional repair. Check watertightness and drying before closing walls or installing cabinets.

Category: Plumbing. Base: EXT-EPA-MOISTURE.

FAQ-054 — Does a fault require renovating the whole kitchen?

No. A fault can be repaired locally if the rest is in good condition. Complete renovation is optional and requires its own scope and quotation.

Category: Kitchens. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-055 — Can I use pipe repairs as an opportunity to renew wall tiles?

Yes. First locate and repair the leak, verify drying and substrate condition; then tile with suitable preparation and a suitable system. Itemise the work separately.

Category: Kitchens. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-056 — Can I also replace cabinets and appliances?

Yes, if the owner chooses and dimensions, water connections, drains, ventilation and electrical circuits are compatible. Replacement is not compulsory because of a leak.

Category: Kitchens. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-057 — Should all old pipes be replaced?

It depends on material, corrosion, leak history, access and remaining sections’ condition. A single leak does not always justify replacing the whole network.

Category: Kitchens. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-058 — Is moving the sink and dishwasher advisable?

It may be feasible, but check water routes, drains, gradients, electricity, ventilation and maintenance access. Retaining positions usually simplifies work.

Category: Kitchens. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-059 — What should be done if water is near sockets?

Do not touch wet energised elements. Isolate supplies only when safe and contact competent professionals if there is risk.

Category: Kitchens. Base: EXT-BOE-REBT.

FAQ-060 — Why does mould return after cleaning?

A moisture or condensation source persists. Identify the cause, repair water ingress or leaks and improve drying or ventilation according to diagnosis.

Category: Damp. Base: EXT-EPA-MOISTURE.

FAQ-061 — How long should I wait before repairing a wet ceiling?

There is no universal repainting interval. First stop the leak and check the substrate is sufficiently dry; EPA advises promptly drying wet materials to reduce mould risk.

Category: Damp. Base: EXT-EPA-GUIDE.

FAQ-062 — Does moisture-resistant board replace shower waterproofing?

No. A shower requires continuous waterproofing with correctly detailed junctions, joints and service penetrations.

Category: Bathrooms. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-063 — What should be checked if a shower leaks?

Drainage, trap, joints, screen, junctions and waterproofing. External silicone does not always correct the source of internal leakage.

Category: Bathrooms. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-064 — Can a bath be replaced by a shower while retaining the drain?

It depends on position, trap, heights, gradients and access. Decide by measuring and checking the actual installation, rather than the catalogue alone.

Category: Bathrooms. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-065 — Can I demolish a partition in an old house?

First establish whether it carries loads, contains services or needs a permit and design. Do not determine load-bearing status from photographs.

Category: Rehabilitation. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-066 — Why does water enter the house after paving the patio?

Gradients, threshold levels, drainage, downpipes and junctions may contribute. Diagnose drainage before adding layers.

Category: Patios. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-067 — Are a concrete floor base and a floor finish the same?

No. The base is part of the supporting construction; the floor finish is the finished flooring. Either can be renewed without changing the other if the substrate is suitable.

Category: Patios. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-068 — Can a kitchen be renovated in phases?

Yes. First resolve faults and decide on services; then finishes and cabinets. Check that dividing work does not require repeated demolition.

Category: Quotations. Base: EXT-NARI-FAQ.

FAQ-069 — What happens with an unforeseen issue behind wall tiles?

Document it, explain the cause and alternatives, measure and price additional work before continuing where possible.

Category: Quotations. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-070 — Can a kitchen price be given without knowing its area?

Not a reliable quotation. First establish area, scope, layout, service condition, materials and access. Do not automatically use the 7 m² example.

Category: Quotations. Base: EXT-NARI-FAQ.

FAQ-071 — Can replacing windows affect ventilation?

Yes. If airtightness improves, review air renewal and condensation risk alongside applicable requirements.

Category: Efficiency. Base: EXT-CTE-HS.

FAQ-072 — Can an office be renovated while people work?

Sometimes, with phases, staff protection, dust and noise control, access and service interruptions. Verify premises-specific requirements.

Category: Offices. Base: EXT-NAHB-LIVING.

FAQ-073 — What should be done if suspected asbestos-containing material appears?

Stop work that could disturb it and consult an authorised specialist. Do not remove, cut or sand it yourself.

Category: Safety. Base: EXT-HSE-ASBESTOS.

FAQ-074 — Can a pool be repaired with paint alone?

Water loss requires identifying evaporation, pipes, joints or the shell as the source; do not assume painting resolves it. This is not a confirmed R4R service.

Category: Other spaces. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-075 — What changes when renovating a sports centre?

Public use, escape routes, accessibility, ventilation, drainage, safety, permits and phased execution. Professionals and regulations for the specific use are required.

Category: Other spaces. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-076 — Why avoid concealing a damp wall with plasterboard?

It can hide active water ingress, degrade materials and hinder checks. First study the water source and a compatible construction solution.

Category: Rehabilitation. Base: EXT-CTE-HS.

FAQ-077 — What should be checked before handing over a renovated bathroom?

Leaks, drainage, seals, sanitary fitting operation, ventilation, finishing details, cleaning and agreed outstanding items; retain documentation and warranties.

Category: Handover. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-078 — Can I live in the house during kitchen renovation?

It depends on service interruptions, safety, dust and habitability; plan a temporary food-preparation area and agree hours.

Category: Kitchens. Base: EXT-NAHB-LIVING.

FAQ-079 — Can I ask in English about Spanish renovations?

Yes, but answers must use relevant documents and verifiable citations; language alone does not guarantee good retrieval. Equivalent queries must be compared.

Category: Bilingual. Base: BASE-CANONICA.

FAQ-080 — Does the US lead rule for homes built before 1978 apply in Spain?

No. That date and those obligations belong to US EPA RRP regulations; Spanish work is governed by its own legal requirements and risk assessments.

Category: Safety. Base: EXT-EPA-LEAD.
