Customer service — initial questions, handover and maintenance

What would you ask before assessing a kitchen?

Where it is, what is wrong, whether there is an active leak, approximate dimensions, photos, what should be kept/changed, service age if known, and proposed budget or schedule. Do not request unnecessary personal data.

Which questions help assess a bathroom?

Availability of another toilet, layout, measurements, leaks, shower gradients, ventilation, building access and desired finishes. A visit may reveal hidden constraints.

What should be asked about damp?

When it began, its relationship with rain or water use, location, seasonality, neighbouring damage, previous renovations and ventilation. Do not confuse symptoms with causes.

What should be requested for an office renovation?

Plan or measurements, activity and hours, expected occupancy where applicable, services, access, users with accessibility needs, urgency and operations that must remain functional.

What should the first visit include?

Visual review and agreed measurements, visible-service inventory, authorised photographs, risks, constraints and inspection limits. Do not describe inaccessible elements as checked.

How should renovation actions be prioritised?

First risks to people and active faults; then stability, watertightness and services; next habitability and optional finishes. The precise execution order depends on the work.

What does a quotation with measurable items mean?

It identifies unit, quantity, scope, price and possible exclusions. It enables comparison of alternatives and change management with less ambiguity than an undetailed total.

What happens when something unforeseen arises?

Document the finding, explain alternatives, agree cost/schedule and record authorisation before non-emergency extraordinary work.

How is a repaired installation verified?

Through technical tests and checks appropriate to the system and scope. Keep results and explain use/maintenance to the owner where appropriate.

What information should the client receive on completion?

List of completed work, warranties/conditions, relevant data sheets, equipment manuals, photos of concealed services and any outstanding issues.

How is a subsequent incident handled?

Record location, symptoms, date, photos and circumstances; check its relationship with the work before attributing cause or warranty coverage. Prioritise active risks.

Can AI provide a quotation in seconds?

AI can structure data and initial questions but cannot replace measurements, visits or contractual offers. Avoid precise figures without itemisation and context.
