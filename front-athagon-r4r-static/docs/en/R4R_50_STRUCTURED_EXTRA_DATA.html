Usage rule

These amounts are not Riansares 4R rates or binding offers. They are dated references from market guides and a technical work item. Before quoting, review measurements, condition, access, quality levels, waste, licences, VAT, margin and risks.

Caution criteria

  • A price per m² supports comparison, not quotations without measurements.
  • The CYPE work item describes a specific configuration and does not automatically include all business costs.
  • Market guides aggregate heterogeneous projects.
  • References must retain their date and source.
  • VAT and licences are verified for each transaction.
