## Can internal insulation be installed without losing ventilation?

Yes, with appropriate design, but it changes hygrothermal behaviour and reduces usable floor area. Vapour barriers must be assessed according to the solution and condensation risk, while maintaining adequate ventilation.

## Can new patio tiles be laid over existing ones?

Only if the existing build-up is sound and compatible and the added height, door clearance, falls and drainage remain acceptable. Adding layers can conceal damp problems and raise the floor enough to obstruct a door.

## What is the difference between thermal and acoustic insulation?

Thermal insulation reduces heat transfer; acoustic insulation aims to control sound transmission. Systems and performance are not automatically interchangeable, although some solutions provide both. Selection depends on the objective, substrate and applicable requirements.

## What should be done about longstanding damp in a house with thick walls?

Review the ground and external levels, downpipes, water ingress, salts, ventilation and previous waterproof treatments. Use compatible materials and carry out local tests before stripping back everything. Do not recommend injections, membranes or barriers as universal solutions without evidence.
