## Can replacing windows increase condensation?

If improved airtightness reduces air renewal and ventilation is not reviewed, indoor moisture balance may change. This does not mean new windows are inherently defective: thermal bridges, temperature and ventilation must be analysed. [CTE DB HS](https://www.codigotecnico.org/pdf/Documentos/HS/DBHS.pdf) regulates aspects of health and hygiene where applicable.

## Can internal insulation be installed without losing ventilation?

Yes, with appropriate design, but it changes hygrothermal behaviour and reduces usable floor area. Vapour barriers must be assessed according to the solution and condensation risk, while maintaining adequate ventilation.
