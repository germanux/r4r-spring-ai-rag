## What is the difference between a concrete floor base and a floor finish?

A **concrete floor base** is a base layer or supporting element on which flooring may be laid. A **floor finish** is the walkable floor finish, such as tiles or another suitable material. They are not synonyms; the construction system and intended use determine the appropriate build-up.

## What is the difference between a concrete floor base and a floor finish?

The **concrete floor base** is a layer or base element that carries loads and provides support according to the construction system. The **floor finish** is the final walkable surface, such as tiles or stone laid on a suitable substrate. They are not synonyms: a floor finish can be renewed while retaining a sound concrete base, or a new base may be needed before paving.

### FAQ-067 — Are a concrete floor base and a floor finish the same?

No. The base is part of the supporting construction; the floor finish is the finished flooring. Either can be renewed without changing the other if the substrate is suitable.

### 6.3. Distinction Between Concrete Floor Base and Floor Finish

**Concrete floor base**: prepared concrete or mortar base to receive the paving, with preparation, thicknesses, reinforcement, slopes, and joints.

**Floor tiling**: placement of the final paving on an appropriate base.

A budget may have:

- fewer m² of base than paving;
- different concreting methods;
- the same type of tile on different supports;
- non-comparable costs per m² if mixed items are combined.
