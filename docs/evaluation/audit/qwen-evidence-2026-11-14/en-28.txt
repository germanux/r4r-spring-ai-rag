### 13.8. Wooden Beams

The beams were partially embedded and it was not possible to treat their entire surface. The criterion was to allow the visible face to continue drying.

Avoid:

- impermeable enamel coatings;
- glossy polyurethane finishes;
- fully wrapping the wood with cement, silicone, or waterproof membrane;
- rigidly sealing the wood-lime joint.

Prefer:

- cleaning and vacuuming;
- checking for dryness and condition;
- open-pore wood stain when applicable;
- ventilation via extractor;
- flexible joint or non-rigid junction.

## How is renovation planned around old timber and walls?

Separate maintenance, defects, services and finishes. Check beams, bearings, damp, biological attack and loads where appropriate; do not cover damaged elements merely to hide them. Maintain material compatibility, ventilation and drying capacity. Seek structural assessment for significant deflection, cracks or deformation.

## What should be done if deteriorated roof timber is discovered?

Stop work that may compromise stability, assess the cause of deterioration and request review by a competent professional where structural doubt exists. Do not replace a load-bearing member with an improvised substitute.
